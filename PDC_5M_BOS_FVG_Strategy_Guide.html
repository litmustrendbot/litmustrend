1. Strategy Overview & Core Philosophy

The PDC 5M BOS + FVG Strategy is an asymmetric, trend-continuation algorithmic model engineered to exploit institutional order flow on the 5-Minute chart. Rather than pursuing high win-rate scalping with tight targets, this strategy targets large 1:10 Risk-to-Reward (10R) runners while utilizing daily macro anchors and strict loss-mitigation circuit breakers.

Key Performance Edge: Because every single winning trade generates +10R (+1000% of risk), a win rate of only 13% – 18% produces substantial net profit over time. The strategy historically won in 13 out of 18 months on real MT5 broker tick data, easily absorbing clustering losses.

2. Higher Timeframe Bias: Previous Day Close (PDC)

Trading against the higher timeframe session momentum is strictly prohibited. The daily bias acts as a master directional permission filter:

  • Anchor Price: Calculated daily at 00:00 server rollover as the exact close price of the prior completed Daily (D1) candle (PDC). Rendered as a 2x-thick solid horizontal line across the chart.
  • Bullish Directional Bias (Longs Only): Valid when the 5-Minute close is above PDC (5M Close > PDC). Only Buy setups are evaluated. All Sell signals are rejected.
  • Bearish Directional Bias (Shorts Only): Valid when the 5-Minute close is below PDC (5M Close < PDC). Only Sell setups are evaluated. All Buy signals are rejected.

3. Internal Market Structure: Swing Fractals & BOS

Structural swing points are isolated using symmetrical 3-bar fractal lookbacks on the 5M chart:

  • Swing High Definition: A bar whose High is strictly higher than the 3 bars preceding it and the 3 bars following it (High[3] > High[3±k] for k = 1, 2, 3).
  • Swing Low Definition: A bar whose Low is strictly lower than the 3 bars preceding it and the 3 bars following it (Low[3] < Low[3±k] for k = 1, 2, 3).
  • Bullish BOS: Confirmed when a just-closed 5M candle closes strictly above a verified Swing High (Close[1] > LastSwingHigh and Close[2] <= LastSwingHigh) with Bullish Bias active.
  • Bearish BOS: Confirmed when a just-closed 5M candle closes strictly below a verified Swing Low (Close[1] < LastSwingLow and Close[2] >= LastSwingLow) with Bearish Bias active.

4. Origin Candle Search & Initiating Wick Confirmation

To eliminate false breakouts occurring during low-volume exhaustion, breakout bars must exceed the origin extreme:

  • Bounded Origin Search: The impulse origin is searched backwards strictly between the breakout bar and the previous swing point: Search_Len = Min(30, Breakout_Bar - Swing_Pivot_Bar).
    • Bullish Origin: The candle with the lowest Low within this window.
    • Bearish Origin: The candle with the highest High within this window.
  • Wick Confirmation Rule:
    • Bullish BOS requires: Close[Breakout] > High[Bullish Origin] (Close must exceed upper wick).
    • Bearish BOS requires: Close[Breakout] < Low[Bearish Origin] (Close must exceed lower wick).

5. Entry Refinement: Fair Value Gap (FVG) vs Origin Zone

Once a breakout is confirmed, the entry is refined to tighten the Stop Loss and maximize position sizing:

  • Fair Value Gap (FVG) Check: Scans the 3-candle sequence leading to the breakout:
    • Bullish FVG: Low[k] > High[k + 2]. Entry = Low[k] (Gap low), SL Ref = High[k + 2].
    • Bearish FVG: High[k] < Low[k + 2]. Entry = High[k] (Gap high), SL Ref = Low[k + 2].
  • Fallback to Origin Zone: If no valid FVG formed:
    • Bullish Setup: Entry = High of Origin Candle; SL Ref = Low of Origin Candle.
    • Bearish Setup: Entry = Low of Origin Candle; SL Ref = High of Origin Candle.
  • Risk Distance & Targets:
    Risk_Distance = Max(|Entry - SL_Ref|, 5 * Point)
    Long: SL = Entry - Risk_Distance | TP = Entry + (10.0 * Risk_Distance)
    Short: SL = Entry + Risk_Distance | TP = Entry - (10.0 * Risk_Distance)

6. Order Placement & Fresh BOS Replacement (The Secret Key)

Orders are placed as Limit Orders at the exact Entry price (Buy Limit / Sell Limit):

The Fresh BOS Replacement Rule (Why This Strategy Outperforms):
If a limit order is pending and unfilled, and price continues to push and produces a new confirmed BOS, the EA / script instantly cancels the old pending order and updates the limit order to the new fresh BOS / FVG level.
Rationale: In strong trending markets, price will rarely pull back deep enough to hit old stale levels. By constantly updating the entry to the freshest market structure, the strategy captures the true runners rather than missing them.
  • Order Expiration: If an order is not filled within 25 bars (125 minutes), it is canceled.
  • Adverse Invalidation: If price breaches the Stop Loss level before filling the limit entry, the setup is canceled immediately.
  • Single Position Rule: Only 1 active trade is allowed at a time.

7. Trade Management: Stepped Trailing Stop (No Early Break-Even)

  • No Early Break-Even: The Stop Loss remains at its initial 1R level until the trade achieves at least +9.5R. Moving stops to break-even at 3R or 5R was proven to cut off runners prematurely during normal market pullbacks.
  • Stepped Trailing Logic (Active between +9.5R and +9.9R):
    • At +9.5R reached → Trail Stop Loss to lock in +5.0R profit.
    • At +9.6R reached → Trail Stop Loss to lock in +6.0R profit.
    • At +9.7R reached → Trail Stop Loss to lock in +7.0R profit.
    • At +9.8R reached → Trail Stop Loss to lock in +8.0R profit.
    • At +9.9R reached → Trail Stop Loss to lock in +9.0R profit.
    • At +10.0R reached → Full Take Profit closes trade for +10.0R (+$100 on $10 risk).

8. Daily Circuit Breaker: Max 3 Consecutive Losses per Day

  • Resets to 0 every day at 00:00 server time.
  • If 3 consecutive losses occur within the same day, trading is HALTED for the remainder of that day. Any pending limit orders are canceled.
  • Any winning trade resets the daily loss streak back to 0.
