Connecting Your MT5 Expert Advisor

Follow these 3 quick steps to link MetaTrader 5 to LitmusTrend.

1

Enable WebRequest in MT5

Open MT5 -> Tools -> Options -> Expert Advisors. Check "Allow WebRequest for listed URL" and add your Vercel URL.

2

Download LitmusTrend.mq5

Place the LitmusTrend_EA.mq5 file inside your MT5 MQL5/Experts folder and compile it in MetaEditor.

3

Attach EA & Enter Secret Key

Drag the EA onto your chart, enter your API Secret Key in the inputs tab, and click OK.
